MEDWYN looks after regular blood tests for your parents, at home.

The same trained person visits each month, so your mother or father sees a familiar face at the door, not a stranger. An NABL-accredited partner laboratory runs the tests and signs every report. We explain the results in plain language, and we call you as soon as anything looks wrong.

Made for families who care for their parents, nearby or from another city:

• Book a home visit for one parent or both. Choose a package or single tests, pick a time, and pay per test.
• See the accredited partner labs near your parent’s home, and choose the one you prefer.
• Tell the technician what matters before every visit: hard of hearing, uses a walker, best time to call.
• Follow the technician on the morning of the visit.
• Read each report in plain words, next to the lab’s signed report. A coordinator calls you within 24 hours of every report.
• Elder Mode gives your parent a few big buttons in their own language, with 108 always one tap away.
• Talk to us: a voice assistant for questions about visits. It does not give medical advice; it passes those questions to a person.
• Medwyn One, an optional membership: a discount on every test and no home-collection charge.

What MEDWYN is not: we are not a laboratory, and the app does not give diagnoses. Results are produced and signed by our NABL-accredited partner laboratory. Nothing in the app replaces your doctor. In an emergency, call 108.

Your data: no advertising, no tracking, and we never sell data. You can delete your account at any time, in Profile.
